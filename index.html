Welcome to Arco

Your cello, from the page to your fingers.

This trainer tests whether you can turn a written bass-clef note into the place you would play it on your cello.

  • Read the black note on the five-line staff.
  • Pick a string and tap 0 for open, or 1–4 on a colored finger tape.
  • Correct answers glow green. If you miss, the correct spot lights up and the answer appears.
  • The next note appears automatically. You can also tap Next note to go faster.
  • Start with D + A, then graduate to all four strings and sharps/flats.
